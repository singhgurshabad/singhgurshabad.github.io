Outside the lab, I lead the Panjabi Students’ Union and Sikh Student Association. Community matters to me as much as craftsmanship—whether I’m organizing an event, volunteering at the gurdwara, or playing tabla.

Speaking English, Punjabi, Hindi, and Urdu has shaped how I think about language, perspective, and building technology for different people.

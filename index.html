About

I study Computer Science & Engineering and Cognitive Science at UC Merced, with an expected graduation in 2028. I’m interested in machine learning, software engineering, and computational approaches to understanding people.

My projects range from a local research assistant using retrieval-augmented generation to pose-estimation research and connected hardware. I plan to pursue a PhD in computer science and continue developing my research practice.

Beyond technical work, I lead student organizations, play tabla, and volunteer at the gurdwara. I speak English, Punjabi, Hindi, and Urdu. My Punjabi language and Sikh literature apps bring these interests into my software work.

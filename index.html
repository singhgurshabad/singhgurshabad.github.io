A computer-vision research pipeline for analyzing behavioral video data and driver–co-driver coordination in rally racing.

Connect-N

A configurable Connect 4, 5, and 6 game with player-versus-player and player-versus-AI modes, adjustable board dimensions, and real-time graphics.

Implementation: Depth-limited Minimax for AI decisions, a modular object-oriented C++ structure, and an OpenGL/GLUT interface with mouse interaction.

Smart Medication Monitor

A connected pill-bottle prototype designed to detect bottle events and estimate changes in pill count through weight measurements.

Team design: Seeed XIAO MG24 Sense, a load cell with an HX711 amplifier, optional IMU motion context, BLE communication, and a 3D-printed weighing platform. Calibration and single-pill resolution remain prototype questions; bottle activity does not confirm ingestion.

About

I’m a Computer Science & Engineering and Cognitive Science student at UC Merced, graduating in 2028. I’m interested in software engineering, machine learning, and computational approaches to understanding human behavior.

Studying both disciplines gives me a practical interest in how systems work and how people interact with them. I plan to pursue a PhD in computer science and continue building my research experience.

I was born in Amritsar, grew up in Karnal, and moved to the United States in 2019. I speak English, Punjabi, Hindi, and Urdu. Outside engineering and research, I play tabla and volunteer at the gurdwara.
